# How to use Menumancer

Guides for each section of the app. The same guide opens in the app with the «?» button next to the section title, and «Show on screen» walks you through the steps with highlights.

- [Library](library.md): All your Dota menu backgrounds. This is where you install them into the game, change their music and menu look, and share presets.
- [Create](create.md): A new background in four steps: where the video comes from, which part loops, what music plays and how it's built.
- [Menu customization](menu.md): Your own look for the Dota main menu. The basics are in view: the Play button color, the match search panel and the top bar. The rest (labels, profile, hero figures, pages) is under “More settings” marked beta: it already works but is still being polished. Only the look changes, through the mod file: nothing changes in a match, and the accept-match window, Dota Plus and shards are left alone.
- [Music](music.md): Playlists for your backgrounds. One playlist can go into several backgrounds, and your edits reach all of them.
- [Settings](settings.md): Everything the build needs: where Dota is, FFmpeg, video quality and what the app does.
- [About](about.md): Version, updates, change history, all guides and how to support development.
