# How to use Menumancer

Guides for each section of the app. The same guide opens in the app with the «?» button next to the section title, and «Show on screen» walks you through the steps with highlights. Step-by-step guides for whole tasks (getting started, troubleshooting, FAQ) are in the [handbook](../../handbook/en/README.md).

- [Library](library.md): All your Dota menu backgrounds. This is where you install them into the game, change their music and what the menu shows, and share them.
- [Create](create.md): A new background in four steps: where the video comes from, which part loops, what music plays and how it's built.
- [Dota menu](menu.md): Your own design for the Dota main menu. The basics are in view: the Play button color, the match search panel and the top bar. The rest (labels, profile, hero figures, pages) is under “More settings” marked beta: it already works but is still being polished. Only the design changes, through the background file: nothing changes in a match, and the accept-match window, Dota Plus and shards are left alone.
- [Music](music.md): Playlists for your backgrounds. One playlist can go into several backgrounds, and your edits reach all of them.
- [Scenes](scenes.md): Live backgrounds drawn by the app itself: northern lights, deep space, embers or your own picture with depth. Dota gets an ordinary video that loops without a seam.
- [Settings](settings.md): Everything the build needs: where Dota is, FFmpeg, what every new background starts with, updates and clean Dota.
- [About](about.md): Version, updates, guides, reporting a problem, supporting development and the change history.
