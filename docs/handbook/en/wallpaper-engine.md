# Wallpaper Engine

If you use Wallpaper Engine, Menumancer can turn your wallpapers into Dota menu backgrounds. It only reads them from your PC; it never ships, downloads or uploads anyone's wallpapers.

Menumancer is not affiliated with Wallpaper Engine.

## Video wallpapers

1. Open **Create**. Your video wallpapers are listed under **Wallpaper Engine** (the **Video** filter). The one on your desktop right now has a badge.
2. Pick one and continue as with any video: [Your first background](first-background.md).

Can't find a wallpaper? Under **Advanced: wallpaper by Workshop ID** paste its number or the link to its Steam page. If it isn't downloaded yet, subscribe in Steam and wait for the download.

## Scenes (beta)

Most Workshop wallpapers are scenes rather than videos. Menumancer has its own scene engine that reads them; Wallpaper Engine doesn't even need to run.

- In **Scenes**, the bottom block lists the scenes from your subscriptions. The badge says how well the engine handles each one: **Supported**, **Partly** or **Not supported**. The **Supported** filter hides the last ones.
- Click a scene to bring it into **My scenes**, with the property choices you made for it in Wallpaper Engine. Then tune it and click **Install into Dota**, as in [Live scenes](live-scenes.md).
- Or in **Create**, switch the list to **Scenes**: the app renders the scene to a video, and you pick the loop and music as usual.

What doesn't work yet:

- **3D scenes.** The app says so instead of importing half a scene.
- **Web and application wallpapers.** These are programs, not pictures, and can't become a menu video.

The engine is new and improving with every version. A scene can look slightly different from Wallpaper Engine. After an app update, clicking a scene tile imports it again with the newer engine, and your earlier copy stays in **My scenes**.

## Whose wallpapers are these

Workshop wallpapers belong to their authors, and many are built from other people's art.

- **For yourself, on your own PC**, making a menu background from a wallpaper you're subscribed to is fine.
- **Don't pass the video on.** The app never puts a Workshop video into a ZIP. To share, use **Share preset**: your friend subscribes to the same wallpaper and their Menumancer builds the same background. See [Sharing](sharing.md).
- **Don't publish the result**, such as a pack of backgrounds for download, without the author's permission.
