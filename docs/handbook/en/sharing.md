# Sharing with friends

A friend likes your menu? There are two ways to pass a background on. Which one you can use depends on where the video came from.

## Share preset (works for everything)

A preset is a small ZIP file with your settings: the loop, the frame, the menu style and so on. It has **no video** and none of your own music in it.

1. In **Library**, open the background with **More details**, or finish a build in **Create**.
2. Click **Share preset**.
3. If the video came from a file, paste the link to the page where you got it (`https://…`), so your friend can download the same video. You can leave it empty.
4. **Save preset** and send the file.

Your friend opens it with **Import ZIP** in their Library.

- **From a Workshop wallpaper:** their app asks them to subscribe to the wallpaper in Steam (**Open in Steam**). Once it's downloaded, they import the preset again and get the same background.
- **From a file:** their app asks for the same video: **Open page and pick file** opens your link, then they pick the downloaded file.

Sound from the video stays. Your own music tracks don't travel; your friend adds their own.

## ZIP with video

For videos you're allowed to give away: your own recordings, videos under a free license such as CC0, or with the author's permission. The ZIP contains the actual video and your music, so your friend needs nothing else.

1. In the background's details, click **ZIP with video**.
2. Confirm **Yes, I can share it**.

Backgrounds made from Workshop wallpapers can't be exported this way: the app always shares them as a preset.

## Importing someone else's ZIP

**Import ZIP** checks the whole archive before unpacking it: paths, file types, sizes. From a ZIP made by another mod tool, the app takes only the video and the music; the menu layout files are always Menumancer's own. Still, only import files from people you trust.

## Why the rules

Videos, wallpapers and music belong to their authors. Making a background for yourself from something you have is fine. Handing someone the video itself is copying it, which needs the author's permission. A preset avoids that: everyone gets the video from the source.
