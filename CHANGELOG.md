## 1.3.1

Fixes and safety. The app asks for your language on the first start and shows release notes in it, and the video guide is linked in
the app. Menu customization stays strictly in the menu (Dota Plus, hero pages and matches untouched; “Own hero cards” removed), only
mods built by the app go into Dota, the treasure button is on by default, updates are checked at every start, and many bugs in Apply,
language switching, Create, music and sharing are fixed.

## 1.3.0

Wallpaper Engine scenes come over more faithfully: their own shaders and Workshop effects, scripts, the property choices you made in WE,
video inside a scene, reflections, particles and puppets closer to the original, and four WE demo scenes are available again. 3D scenes
say plainly that they aren't supported yet.

## 1.2.0

Big update: the Scenes section (beta) with the app's own engine. App templates, your own picture with depth and Wallpaper Engine scenes
become menu backgrounds, with a layer and effect editor and a cinema-style frame. A scene's video is checked and tuned to fit 19.2 MB,
and rendering is faster. Loop search by motion, 30 fps by default.

## 1.1.2

The background now shows in the game with English and other voice languages: the `dota_lv` folder and the `-lv` launch option, and a
question about the voice language before the first install.

## 1.1.1

A new version of the license agreement (1.1): the app shows it at the first start after the update.

## 1.1.0

Big update: Dota menu customization (beta), hero figures, a menu look of its own for every background, music on the background card
(beta), section guides, Czech language. “Recipes” are now called presets.

## 1.0.0

The first public version.
